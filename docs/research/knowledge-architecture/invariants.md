# Knowledge Architecture Candidate Invariants

This ledger accumulates evidence-backed **candidate** invariants during the Knowledge Architecture Evidence Campaign. These are not final architecture rules.

## Status meanings

- **candidate** — supported by one bounded task.
- **reinforced** — independently supported by multiple tasks.
- **qualified** — retained with narrower scope after counterevidence.
- **rejected** — evidence showed the formulation was too strong or wrong.

## KA-11 Agno revisit update

- KA-11 was promoted by the bounded coverage scan specifically to test under-covered persistent-knowledge ownership and mutation authority.
- **KA-I-005 moved candidate → reinforced.** Current Agno EntityMemory independently preserves same-name/type ambiguity, supports qualified disambiguation, refuses ambiguous mutations/links and explicitly prefers recoverable fragmentation over an irreversible wrong merge.
- **KA-I-034 moved candidate → reinforced.** Agno independently distinguishes user-private, global/shared and custom knowledge namespaces; current AgentOS binds authenticated user scope at routes; #9983 and historical #8334 demonstrate concrete consequences when owner scope is dropped from mutation/identity.
- **KA-I-043 moved candidate → reinforced.** Current Agno separates add/update/delete/clear controls, labels clear as dangerous/default-disabled in the newer UserMemoryConfig, and #9983 demonstrates why a database-global clear cannot inherit ordinary user-memory authority.
- KA-I-004 remains candidate: Agno source explicitly states wrong merges have no general unmerge; avoidance/ambiguity handling is not reversible merge/split lineage.
- KA-I-007 remains candidate: Agno stores first-class relationship edges but relation labels remain open strings without governed consequence semantics.
- KA-I-031, KA-I-037–040 and KA-I-044–045 remain candidates; Agno supplied adjacent but not complete independent matches.
- KA-11 adds strong recurrence for KA-I-003, 006, 009, 012, 014–020, 023–024, 027–029 and 035, particularly through authenticated route scoping, fact retirement, explicit migrations and copy/read-verify/delete replacement.
- No invariant is promoted to a final architecture rule during the evidence campaign.
