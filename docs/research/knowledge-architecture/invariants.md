# Knowledge Architecture Candidate Invariants

This ledger accumulates evidence-backed **candidate** invariants during the Knowledge Architecture Evidence Campaign. These are not final architecture rules.

## Status meanings

- **candidate** — supported by one bounded task.
- **reinforced** — independently supported by multiple tasks.
- **qualified** — retained with narrower scope after counterevidence.
- **rejected** — evidence showed the formulation was too strong or wrong.

## KA-G2 update

- **KA-I-007 moved candidate → reinforced.** OWL, SKOS, Wikibase and OBO independently show that relation identity and governed semantics such as direction, inverse, transitivity, cardinality, domain/range, constraints and relation reuse are behavior-bearing and cannot safely be inferred from a text label alone.
- **KA-I-048 is new, candidate.** RDF 1.2 and Wikibase independently distinguish an abstract relationship proposition from separately identifiable statement/reifier occurrences carrying source, qualifiers, rank and context.
- **KA-I-049 is new, candidate.** SKOS, OWL, SPARQL and RDF entailment semantics distinguish direct assertions, inferred closure and path connectivity; consequential answers need explainable support from asserted edges and an identifiable inference/vocabulary profile.
- KA-G2 adds strong recurrence for KA-I-002, 005–006, 008–009, 012, 017–018, 022–023, 025 and 030.
- KA-I-004 remains candidate: standards expose identity hazards from functional/inverse-functional semantics but do not supply an independently validated reversible merge/split mechanism.
- KA-I-039 remains candidate: Wikibase delayed schema migration is adjacent evidence, not an independent serialization-fidelity match.
- No invariant is promoted to a final architecture rule during this evidence task.
